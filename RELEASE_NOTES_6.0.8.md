# MKV Encoder Q 6.0.8

In Encoder, select adjacent rows with Shift-click and drag any selected row by its left handle. The group keeps its order. For a selection with gaps, use the right-click **Move to Top** or **Move to Bottom** command.

Right-click a waiting file and choose **Move Folder to Top** or **Move Folder to Bottom** to move eligible rows from its exact folder, including rows hidden by filters. Files in subfolders keep their positions relative to other rows outside the group. Active encodes continue running.
